# Submission description (paste-ready)

**Riaya (رعاية): a private, Arabic-first front door to men's reproductive health in Abu Dhabi**

**Live:** https://riaya-xhby.onrender.com · **Code and docs:** https://github.com/yugesh1111/riaya · **Apps:** Android APK and Mac DMG on the release page

## Every judge remark has been addressed

1. **"Private health companion with explainable risk insights and safe next steps"**
   - Every finding explains what we saw, why it matters, the evidence grade (A–D, 95 sources), what helps, and its limits.
   - Safe next steps: now, next weeks, 3 months, "please don't", and when to get help sooner.
   - Ask about your summary, with guardrails: urgent symptoms → 998, no doses, no diagnosis.
2. **"Connected referral journey to specialist consultation and follow-up"**
   - The doctor approves every referral, then he books and can call or WhatsApp the clinic in one tap.
   - The specialist opens the referral with a one-time code and returns the visit summary.
   - Deadlines by urgency and escalation, published as a FHIR R4 Task; follow-up and a re-check at 3 months.
3. **"Clear explanations, user-controlled privacy, consent-based sharing"**
   - He chooses what each recipient sees and previews the letter word for word.
   - One switch pauses all sharing.
   - He gets an access alert every time a doctor opens his record, plus consent receipts.
   - He chooses how long data is kept.

## The organisers' Wellbeing Q&A: all 10 questions covered
- COM-B and BCT behaviour design, just-in-time nudges, a fresh start after a lapse, and Ramadan mode.
- WHO-5 at day 0, 30 and 84 (10-point meaningful change), and engagement at weeks 4, 8 and 12.
- Closed-loop referral tracking (FHIR Task), clinician in the loop, explainable AI for patients, doctors and regulators.
- Local evidence from the UAE and Gulf: STOP-Bang validated in Arabic in Al Ain, Arabic WHO-5, the UAE National Health Survey, and Abu Dhabi semen data.

## What makes Riaya different
1. **Calculator cover and locked by default:** it opens as a working calculator; his PIN then "=" opens Riaya.
2. **Talk it through in Gulf Arabic or English**, in four Arabic accents; it can understand speech on the phone itself.
3. **Encrypted vault:** reports never leave his phone, and each one asks for Face ID again.
4. **Gym product checker:** a photo of a label flags testosterone, steroids and SARMs.
5. **Prescriptive and predictive:** his best 3 changes, a 12-week projection, and lab trends forecast to the next test.
6. **UAE heat guard and Watch first** (Apple Watch, Wear OS, Garmin).
7. **Impact simulator and a k-anonymised programme dashboard** for the health authority.
8. **Runs everywhere:** iPhone, Android, Mac, WhatsApp; doctors use the same app.
